Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
================================================================

This is a reference file for the CC BY 4.0 license used by the Twemoji and
Font Awesome Free icon sets bundled in this project.

The authoritative legal text is published by Creative Commons and must be
read from the official source — it is not reproduced here:

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

What the license requires of this project (summary — the legal text governs):

  - Appropriate credit: name of the creator, copyright notice, license
    notice, disclaimer notice, and a link to the licensed material.
  - Indication of changes: if the material was modified, that must be stated.
  - No additional restrictions: you may not apply terms that restrict what
    the license otherwise permits.

The required attribution for each CC BY pack bundled here is given in
THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md (see the "Required attribution" section).
